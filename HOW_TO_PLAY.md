# How to Play Unhinged

Two Leaders. One bad week. Somebody's about to lose it.

You win by making the other Leader come completely **Unhinged**. Every Leader starts at **20 Composure**. Your Characters wear it down by **Causing Trouble**. Their Characters fight yours. Somewhere around 10 Composure, things get weird. At 0, things get worse.

This is everything you need for a first game. When something strange happens, check the [Rules Reference](RULES.md).

## What you need

- A 40-card deck, its **Leader**, and one hidden **Last Straw** card.
- Something to track Composure (a d20 works) and damage (small dice or tokens).
- A d6. Some decks really like dice.

## Setup

1. Put your Leader face up in front of you at **20 Composure**.
2. Slide your chosen **Last Straw** face down under your Leader. Don't show anyone.
3. Shuffle your deck. Play **War** to see who goes first: each player flips their top card, higher Cost wins, flip again on a tie. Shuffle the flipped cards back in.
4. Draw 7. You may mulligan any number of them once: set them aside, draw that many, then shuffle the set-aside cards into your deck.

The first player skips their Draw on their very first turn.

## Reading a card

- **Cost** (top left): how much Stash you spend to play it.
- **Characters** have three numbers:
  - **Power:** damage it deals when it Attacks.
  - **Health:** damage it can take. When damage on it reaches its Health, it's **Defeated**.
  - **Trouble:** Composure the opposing Leader loses when it **Causes Trouble**.
- **Actions** happen once, then go to your discard.
- **Items** stay in play. Some attach to one of your Characters.

## Ready and Exposed

Cards are either **Ready** (upright) or **Exposed** (turned sideways). Acting turns a Character sideways, and that leaves it **Exposed**: opposing Characters can Attack it. A Ready Character is protected from Attacks.

That's the whole tension of the game. Every time you act, you put someone in harm's way.

Items and Stash turn sideways too. For them, it's called **Used**.

## Your turn

1. **Ready** everything you have: Characters, Items and Stash.
2. **Draw** a card.
3. **Do stuff**, in any order, as much as you can afford:
   - **Stash a card**, once per turn: put a card from your hand face down in your Stash. It's your money from now on.
   - **Play cards** by Using that much Stash. Each Ready Stash card pays 1.
   - **Use your Characters.** Each Ready Character may do one of three things.

## The three choices

**Attack.** Pick an **Exposed** opposing Character. Your Character becomes Exposed and deals damage equal to its Power. There's no blocking, and the target doesn't hit back unless it has **Hits Back**. Damage stays on a Character until it's healed or leaves play.

**Cause Trouble.** Your Character becomes Exposed, and the opposing Leader loses Composure equal to its Trouble. Trouble can't be blocked. A Character needs at least 1 Trouble to do this.

**Stay Ready.** Do nothing, stay upright, and stay safe from Attacks.

**New arrivals:** a Character you play this turn can't Attack or Cause Trouble until your next turn. **Hothead** lets it Attack right away, but it still can't Cause Trouble yet.

## The three acts

**Act 1: 20 Composure.** Normal life. Build a board and start causing problems.

**Act 2: Breaking Point (10 or less).** The first time a Leader drops to 10 or less, its printed **Breaking Point** fires. Every Leader cracks differently, and it only happens once.

**Act 3: Last Straw (0).** Reaching 0 doesn't lose the game. Flip your hidden **Last Straw** card and do what it says. Then the current turn ends. While you're at Last Straw:

- your Characters have **Hothead** and may Attack opposing **Ready** Characters too;
- the **next** time an opposing Character Causes Trouble against you, you're **Unhinged**, and you lose.

> **Paper-test variant — Last Straw Guard.** While you're at Last Straw, opponents can't Cause Trouble while you have a Ready Character, and your Ready Characters can be Attacked. Clear the guards, then go for the finish.

## Keywords

- **Hothead:** may Attack the turn it's played.
- **Sucker Punch:** may Attack Ready Characters, not just Exposed ones.
- **Hits Back:** when it's Attacked, it deals its Power back to the attacker, even if it was Defeated.
- **Thick Skin X:** each time it's dealt damage, reduce that damage by X. Doesn't stack; use the highest.
- **Meat Shield:** while it's Exposed, opponents must Attack it before any other Character. You may play it Exposed.
- **Explosive:** when it's Defeated, deal 1 damage to each opposing Character.
- **Stubborn:** the first time each Round it would be Returned or Dismissed, it stays.
- **Jerry-Rig:** if this Item would go to your discard, you may put it face up and Used in your Stash instead. Discard it when you spend it.

## Three golden rules

1. **The card beats the rulebook.** If a card says to do something the rules say you can't, do what the card says.
2. **Finish what you're doing, then check Composure.** Resolve the whole effect first, then any Breaking Point, then any Last Straw.
3. **If two things happen at once, the player whose turn it is picks the order.**
