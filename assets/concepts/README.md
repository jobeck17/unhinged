# Historical Visual Concepts

These images preserve earlier visual explorations. They are not approved card art, a locked card back, or the current card template.

- [Trailer Park Junction](Unhinged%20Trailer%20Park%20Junction.png) and [Golden T-Junction Card Back](Unhinged%20Golden%20T-Junction%20Card%20Back.png) explore the tabled road or map card-back idea.
- [Cards Bigger Plays](Unhinged%20Cards%3A%20Bigger%20Plays.png) is an early presentation concept.

The current [card design principles](../../docs/card-design-principles-2026-09-22.md) prioritize readable information in a hand and on the table. The [idea bank](../../docs/idea-bank.md) records the card-back experiment and why bluffing is the more promising use of hidden cards.
