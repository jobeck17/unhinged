# Locked Salvage set — October 13, 2026

32 active designs: 18 Characters / 6 Actions / 8 Items. Handyman parked October 13; other approved mechanics retained. Economy and disposable-Item variety under review; production implementation pending.

Character stats: Power / Health / Trouble.

Trash Baron passive: You may use opposing Ready Stash to pay your Costs as though it were your own. If an Item you control would go from play to your discard, you may put it face-up and Rotated into your Stash instead. Discard it when spent.

Breaking Point: At the start of your next turn, Ready all opposing Stash.

Scurry Away: When an opposing Character would Cause Trouble to your Leader, you may return this Character to your hand to reduce that Trouble by 1.
