## Timing and balance checks

- Strong Character Dismiss rewards are once during your turn per copy; multiple copies still stack and require testing. No turn-wide global cap is assumed.
- Hoarder discount is capped at 1 across all Hoarder copies on the first Item played during your turn (locked October 10).
- Scrap redirects a qualifying play-to-discard movement. Actual Dismiss still counts once; spending or cashing in Stash is not Dismiss.
- Temporary replay tracks the actual played instance. Leaving and returning does not transfer its end-turn Dismiss to a new instance. No replay of an Item already in play.
- Ready-all Breaking Point may give substantial fuel during the opponent turn, but there are no general response windows. Readying Stash does not create a response action.
- Superseding October 10 lock: Scurry Away returns its Character from play to hand when an opposing Character would Cause Trouble to your Leader, reducing that Trouble by 1. Multiple actual returns may stack; minimum Trouble zero. Multiple activated Carts may also stack on the same next event; unused reduction does not carry forward. Rat King's separate permanent reduction remains draft text to revisit, not locked.
- Damage protection reduces normal damage, not Dismiss, Defeat effects, or Return. Absorb order and stacking need implementation review.
- Zip Ties and Used Ham Sandwich deliberately lack Scrap: recovery has real discard targets and not every tool generates fuel.
- No Ready-Scrap override or permanent Item discount. No free recursive replay in this draft.
- Raccoon + disposable Item supports combat; Rat King + Busted Shopping Cart supports bounded Trouble defense; Garage Inventor/Quartermaster/Cat Lady reward distinct outputs.
- Item activation legality must be checked before Dismiss/payment. Rusty Toolbox cannot recover itself because its target must already be in discard and be another Item.
- All 30 original slots accounted for; two new Items added. Pool Guy original ability/name banked with Florida Man flag. New names remain provisional.

## Scurry Away — superseding October 10 lock

**When an opposing Character would Cause Trouble to your Leader, you may return this Character to your hand to reduce that Trouble by 1.**

This replaces the old Defeated/Item-Dismiss recovery definition wherever the keyword appears. Ready and Rotated Characters qualify. It does not cover general Composure loss or introduce generic response windows. Each reduction requires an actual return and multiple Characters may stack. At least one 1-cost pest should carry it; individual assignments remain for card review. Original Possum recovery can be retained as explicit text if subsequently approved. Cart's paid-for reductions may stack on the same event and combine with Scurry Away; minimum zero, no leftover protection carried into later events.

## October 9 interview lock

Cash In the Clutter is locked at cost 2 with the Trouble conversion above. Ready or Rotated face-up Items qualify. Moving them from Stash is not Dismiss and does not trigger Item-Dismiss rewards. Make It Work Dismisses from play: an Item with Scrap may become face-up Rotated temporary Stash instead of entering discard, and still counts as Dismissed once. An Item without Scrap goes to discard. This clarifies the locked Scrap direction; it does not individually lock the Make It Work draft.

Busted Shopping Cart cost reduced to 2 and locked October 9. Draw direction: modest card advantage, primarily through Item finding/revealing and recovery/recycling; Oswald and Tamatoa are user references. Generic draw effects in this draft must be revisited, not automatically removed.

Garage Inventor discover-and-deploy ability locked October 9. Once during your turn per copy; the use is spent even on a miss. Entry effects still resolve on Rotated Items. Recommended wording clarification for review: put the revealed card on the bottom if a qualifying free play is declined or illegal. Cost/stats remain draft values.

Garage Inventor update: Joseph removed the Item cost limit. Any revealed Item can be deployed free Rotated; once during your turn remains.

Trans Am ability locked: entry Item recovery plus once during your turn recovery of an actual Item spent from your own Stash. Cash In does not qualify. Cost/stats/name remain for individual review.

Magnet on a String retrieval revision locked October 9: another face-up Item from your own Stash to hand. Ready or Rotated target qualifies. Validate target before activation; cannot retrieve itself. Stash-to-hand is not a Character return or Item Dismiss. Name remains provisional.

Make It Work locked October 11: cost 2; Dismiss one of your Items to Draw 2 cards, no discard. Broad pre-card-review concerns are complete. Other conventional draw abilities remain for individual card review. Requested next-stopping-point play-line review of finished decks remains outstanding before the Salvage card-by-card pass.
