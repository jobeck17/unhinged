> **Status: brainstorm / concept bank. Not production card text and not additions to the current 180-card pool.**

## Strong early candidates

### Miss Tammy, Lot 14
Neighborhood intelligence broker. Good home for looking at hidden information, face-down cards, top cards, or otherwise knowing things she should not know. Could overlap the emerging Spy package while remaining an ordinary resident.

### Lady Who's Moving Out Again
Especially strong fit for Misdirection. Repeatedly Returning herself to hand could make her part of the Magician bounce/replay engine without requiring every card in that engine to have the Magician Trait.

### Guy Fixing His Trans Am
Potential Salvage build-around centered on Items, discarded/Dismissed material, Stash, and assembling a payoff over time. The eventual “Trans Am is running” moment should feel earned and ridiculous.

## Worldbuilding direction

The trailer park should function as a recurring Unhinged setting rather than a siloed archetype. Residents can appear across all six Styles and retain normal Traits such as Parent, Criminal, Builder, Kid, Scavenger, etc. Avoid creating a Trailer Park Trait merely to group them.

When production slots are revisited, prefer replacing weaker/generic concepts in the current pool with the strongest members of this bank rather than automatically expanding beyond 180 cards.
