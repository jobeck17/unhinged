## Core Mechanic Direction

### Junk Pile
Your Junk Pile is a face-up zone with a maximum of **3 cards**.

Once each round, when one of your cards is dismissed or one of your Units dies, you may put that card into your Junk Pile instead of your discard. If your Junk Pile is full, you must dismiss a card from it before adding another.

Cards in the Junk Pile have **no inherent effect and generate no Resource**. Makeshift cards and Leaders may specifically use, move, play, or care about cards in Junk.

### Current Junk Actions
- **Repurpose** — Mill 2 cards. You may put an Item from your discard into your Junk Pile.
- **Salvage** — Return a card from your Junk Pile to your hand.
- **Waste Not** — If the opponent ended their last round with unused resources, put the top card of your deck into your Junk Pile.

The goal is a visible, capped synergy zone rather than a second wallet or hidden graveyard extension.

**Shark** is also planned as a deliberately plain 3-cost 3/3 Unit so the laser-beam Item has a straight-faced host.
