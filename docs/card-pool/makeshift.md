# Makeshift Card Pool

**Identity:** scrappy value, Items, scavenging, recycling, tokens.  
**Economy:** waste is fuel.

## Leader

**Trash Baron** — 21 Health

Current direction: scavenging, Vermin, recursion, and Repurpose/Scrap synergy. Exact passive and active wording remain provisional.

## Actions (5)

1. **Scrounge** — Draw a card. If an Item entered your discard this round, draw 2 instead.
2. **I Can Fix That** — Return an Item from your discard to your hand.
3. **Found This in the Garage** — Reveal the top three cards of your deck. Put an Item among them into your hand.
4. **Good as New** — Return a Unit costing 2 or less from your discard to your hand.
5. **That's Probably Still Good** — Return an Item from your discard to play attached to an eligible Unit. Dismiss it at end of round. When it is dismissed this round, draw a card.

## Core Mechanic Direction

### Repurpose
Once per round, place a card from your discard face down in your Scrap row.

### Scrap
Face-down Scrap has no printed characteristics. Current direction: dismiss Scrap to generate temporary resource usable on Makeshift cards and/or Items.

### Salvage
Reveal a face-down Scrap card and return it to your hand.

Possible extension: if the opponent ends a round with unused resources, Makeshift gets an additional Repurpose opportunity.

**Shark** is also planned as a deliberately plain 3-cost 3/3 Unit so the laser-beam Item has a straight-faced host.
