# Mongo Meta Audit — 2026-09-19

> **Important:** Heuristic optimized-deck stress test, not a rules-complete engine. For this pass, **rank/color clustering is more important than the exact win percentage**.

## Kamikaze dependency + Unruly micro-buff pass

Kamikaze generic payoffs were moved behind **Undead / Fanatic / Daredevil / Saboteur / Florida Man** conditions on Detonate, Blood Price, This Seemed Like a Good Idea, Worth It, Bong Water, and Dead Man's Switch.

Unruly received four small lifts: Social Media Influencer grants +1/+1 for the round, Roll Call turns on at two Units, Pep Talk is +2/+2, and Participation Trophy grants +1 Power when it moves after surviving combat.

## Read

The previous Kamikaze wall has broken apart. Kamikaze no longer occupies all ten of the top eleven slots, and Unruly is no longer almost entirely buried at the bottom. The field is not perfectly uniform, nor should it be. This pass is aimed at restoring meaningful color choice rather than forcing every combination toward 50%.
