# Reckless Card Pool

**Identity:** risk, aggression, burst Power, self-damage.  
**Economy:** pain is fuel.

## Units (20)

1. **Feral Chihuahua** — 1 cost, 2/1 — *Animal • Menace* — Vanilla.
2. **Bachelorette Party** — 2 cost, 3/1 — *Human • Party* — When this attacks, another Party Unit gets +1 Power this round.
3. **Gas Station Daredevil** — 2 cost, 2/2 — *Human • Florida Man • Daredevil* — Whenever you dismiss an Item, this gets +1 Power this round.
4. **Pirate With No Exit Strategy** — 3 cost, 4/2 — *Human • Outlaw* — When this attacks, you may discard a card. If you do, it gets +2 Power this attack.
5. **Put It in Reverse, Terry!** — 3 cost, 4/3 — *Human • Menace* — The first time this would take combat damage while attacking each round, you may return it to your hand instead.
6. **Washed-Up Rock Star** — 3 cost, 4/3 — *Human • Party • Performer* — Gets +1 Power while you control another Party Unit.
7. **Unsupervised Toddler** — 2 cost, 1/3 — *Human • Menace* — When this attacks, reveal the top card of your deck. If it is a Unit, Toddler gets +2 Power this round. Otherwise, exhaust it after combat.
8. **Gas Station Gladiator** — 5 cost, 6/4 — *Human • Daredevil* — Whenever this survives combat while attacking, draw a card.
9. **Firework Dad** — 2 cost, 3/2 — *Human • Daredevil • Menace* — When this attacks, you may deal 1 damage to it. If you do, it gets +2 Power this attack.
10. **Karaoke Champion** — 2 cost, 2/2 — *Human • Party* — Gets +1 Power while another Party Unit is attacking.
11. **Petty Thief** — 2 cost, 3/1 — *Human • Outlaw* — When this damages the enemy Leader, draw a card, then discard a card.
12. **Backyard Wrestler** — 3 cost, 4/3 — *Human • Daredevil* — Whenever this survives combat while attacking, ready it. It cannot attack again this round.
13. **Road Rage Ron** — 4 cost, 5/3 — *Human • Menace* — When deployed, an enemy Unit loses 1 Guard this round.
14. **Pool Pirate** — 4 cost, 4/4 — *Human • Outlaw • Party* — Whenever another Outlaw or Party Unit attacks, this gets +1 Power this round.
15. **Bottle-Rocket Enthusiast** — 4 cost, 5/4 — *Human • Daredevil* — When this dies during your turn, deal 1 damage to a Unit.
16. **Guy Who Definitely Read the Instructions** — 5 cost, 7/4 — *Human • Florida Man* — When this attacks, dismiss one of your Items or deal 2 damage to this.
17. **Fourth of July Showrunner** — 4 cost, 5/3 — *Human • Party • Daredevil* — When another Daredevil attacks, you may deal 1 damage to it. If you do, it gets +2 Power this attack.
18. **Parking Lot Boxer** — 3 cost, 4/3 — *Human • Menace* — When this attacks a damaged Unit, it gets +1 Power this attack.
19. **Pirate Captain of Questionable Credentials** — 5 cost, 6/5 — *Human • Outlaw* — Other Outlaws get +1 Power while attacking.
20. **Guy on a Rental Scooter** — 1 cost, 2/1 — *Human • Daredevil* — When this dies during your turn, draw a card, then discard a card.

## Actions (5)

1. **Make a Scene** — A Unit gets +2 Power this round.
2. **Hold My Beer** — A Unit gets +3 Power this attack. Deal 1 damage to that Unit after combat.
3. **Send It!** — A Unit gets +3 Power this attack and -1 Guard this round.
4. **Fireworks Incident** — Deal 2 damage to one of your Units and 2 damage to an enemy Unit.
5. **No, No, Watch This** — Ready a Unit that attacked this round. It cannot attack again this round. It gets +2 Power until end of round.

## Items (5)

1. **Bath Salts** — Attach to a Unit. Attached Unit gains *Undead*. Dismiss: it gets +2 Power this round.
2. **Gas Station Pills** — Attach to a Unit. +1 Power. When dismissed, ready attached Unit, then deal 1 damage to it.
3. **Roman Candle** — Attach to a Unit. The first time attached Unit attacks each round, deal 1 damage to an enemy Unit, then dismiss Roman Candle.
4. **Cooler Full of Bad Ideas** — Dismiss: discard a card, then draw 2.
5. **Illegal Firework** — Attach to a Unit. When attached Unit attacks, you may dismiss this to deal 2 damage to any Unit.

## Notes

Reckless should increasingly use **self-damage as an alternate cost**. Some future Actions may be deliberately undercosted if they damage the Leader or the acting Unit.
