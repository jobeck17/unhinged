# Stubborn Card Pool

**Identity:** resilience, Guard, blocking, prevention, refusing to move.  
**Economy:** patience and adversity create efficiency.

3. **HOA Enforcement Committee** — 3 cost, 3/5 — *Human • Authority* — When deployed, an enemy Unit gets -1 Power this round.

## Actions (5)

1. **File a Complaint** — An attacking Unit gets -2 Power this attack.
2. **Not in My Neighborhood** — Move an attacking Unit out of combat unless its controller pays an additional resource. Exact cost TBD.
3. **Absolutely Not** — An attacking Unit loses all Power bonuses this attack.
4. **I Want to Speak to Your Manager** — Return an Action targeting one of your Units to its owner's hand instead of resolving it. Timing wording TBD.
5. **Read the Fine Print** — Cancel a Power or Guard bonus granted by an enemy card this round.

### Additional current-pool Action

- **Dig In** — Costs 1 less if you control fewer Units than the opponent. Your Units get +1 Guard this round. If you control fewer Units than the opponent, draw a card.

## Items (5)

1. **Security Camera** — The first enemy Unit to attack your Leader each round gets -1 Power.
2. **Rabies Shot** — Dismiss: remove all damage from an Animal, then it loses its abilities this round.
3. **Ring Doorbell** — Whenever an enemy Unit attacks your Leader for the first time each round, draw a card, then discard a card.
4. **Decorative Boulder** — Attach to a Unit. +2 Guard. Attached Unit cannot be moved by enemy effects.
5. **Folding Lawn Chair** — Attach to a Unit. Attached Unit gets +2 Guard while blocking.

## Notes

Stubborn's resource identity should reward **waiting or being behind**:

- costs less if you have not attacked this round
- costs less if the opponent has more Units
- costs less if your Leader has less health
- costs less after your Leader was attacked

The color should become harder to finish as adversity increases, without simply receiving oversized stats for free.
