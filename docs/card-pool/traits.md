# Unhinged Traits

> **LEGACY ALPHA 0.03 CARD POOL.** This sheet reflects the old test data, including retired wording. Use the [latest checkpoint](../current-state-2026-09-21.md), [LAB rules](rules-lab.md), and [terminology](../terminology.md) for current design. The cards need a deliberate rewrite before reuse.

## Trait Rules

- Every Leader currently has **exactly 4 Traits**.
- Any Unit, Action, or Item may have **0–3 Traits**.
- Traits are allowed on Actions and Items; they are not Unit-only labels.
- Gameplay Traits should normally appear in at least **2 colors**, with some concentrated heavily in a primary color.
- **Human** and **Animal** are broad identity Traits and are not balance targets in the same way as the gameplay tribes.
- `Vehicle`, `Object`, and `Coach` were removed from the active Trait pool in this pass because they were one-card / one-Leader islands rather than useful deckbuilding hooks.

## Important Bridge Changes

- **Undead** is now a **Makeshift + Kamikaze** bridge instead of Stubborn + Kamikaze. Cockroach and Possum in the Trash Can now carry Undead into Makeshift.
- **Hacker** now appears outside ROOT/Crooked, especially on Makeshift tech cards and Stubborn surveillance Items.
- Actions and Items now materially contribute to Trait density, so tribal packages are not forced to be Unit-only.
