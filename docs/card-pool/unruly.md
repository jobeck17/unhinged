# Unruly Card Pool

**Identity:** teamwork, momentum, deployment, buffs, bodies.  
**Economy:** people create momentum.

## Units (20)

1. **Little Leaguer** — 2 cost, 2/3 — *Human • Team*.
2. **Girl Scout** — 2 cost, 2/2 — *Human • Team • Hustler* — When deployed, draw a card, then discard a card.
3. **MLM Rep** — 2 cost, 2/2 — *Human • Hustler* — Whenever you deploy another Hustler, this gets +1 Power this round.
4. **Birthday Party Magician** — 3 cost, 2/4 — *Human • Performer* — When deployed, create a 1/1 Rabbit.
5. **Wedding Cover Band** — 3 cost, 3/3 — *Human • Party • Performer* — Other Party Units get +1 Guard while this is ready.
6. **Little League Assistant Coach** — 4 cost, 4/5 — *Human • Team* — Vanilla.
7. **Social Media Influencer** — 3 cost, 3/3 — *Human • Hustler* — Whenever another Unit enters your field, you may give it +1 Power this round.
8. **Jam Band Hippie** — 4 cost, 3/5 — *Human • Performer • Fanatic* — When another Performer or Fanatic is deployed, heal 1 damage from a Unit.
9. **Soccer Mom** — 2 cost, 2/3 — *Human • Team* — When another Team Unit enters your field, it gets +1 Guard this round.
10. **Dance Mom** — 2 cost, 2/2 — *Human • Team • Hustler* — Whenever you buff another Unit, this gets +1 Power this round.
11. **Girl Scout Troop Leader** — 3 cost, 3/4 — *Human • Team • Hustler* — When deployed, look at the top two cards of your deck. You may reveal a Team Unit and put it into your hand.
12. **Wedding DJ** — 3 cost, 3/3 — *Human • Party • Performer* — When deployed, another Unit gets +1 Power and +1 Guard this round.
13. **Youth Pastor With a Guitar** — 3 cost, 2/4 — *Human • Performer • Fanatic* — Whenever another Fanatic or Performer enters, heal 1 damage from this.
14. **Stage Mom** — 4 cost, 3/5 — *Human • Hustler • Performer* — Your 1-cost and 2-cost Units get +1 Power during your turn.
15. **Single Dad** — 4 cost, 4/5 — *Human • Team* — When deployed, another Unit you control gets +2 Guard this round.
16. **Neighborhood Block Party** — 5 cost, 5/6 — *Party • Team* — Other Party Units get +1 Power. *(Card type may change later.)*
17. **Little League Umpire's Kid** — 2 cost, 2/3 — *Human • Team* — Gets +1 Power while you control a Coach.
18. **Dance Recital Dad** — 3 cost, 3/4 — *Human • Team • Performer* — Whenever another Performer gets a buff, this gets +1 Guard this round.
19. **Girl Scout Cookie Captain** — 4 cost, 4/5 — *Human • Team • Hustler* — When deployed, search the top four cards of your deck for a Team or Hustler Unit and put it into your hand.
20. **Wedding Singer** — 4 cost, 4/4 — *Human • Party • Performer* — Whenever another Party Unit attacks, you may give it +1 Guard this round.

## Actions (5)

1. **Group Text** — Draw 2, then discard 1.
2. **Everybody Bats** — Up to three Units you control get +1 Power this round.
3. **Carpool** — Return one of your Units to hand. Deploy a Unit costing the same or less for 1 less.
4. **Everybody Gets a Turn** — Up to three Units get +1 Guard this round.
5. **Rally the Group Chat** — Choose a Trait. Up to three Units you control with that Trait get +1 Power this round.

## Items (5)

1. **Participation Trophy** — Attached Unit gets +1 Guard. When it survives combat, you may move this Item to another Unit.
2. **Hacky Sack** — Exhaust: another Performer gets +1 Power and +1 Guard this round.
3. **Orange Slices** — Dismiss: heal 2 damage from a Team Unit.
4. **Portable Bluetooth Speaker** — Exhaust: a Party or Performer gets +1 Power this round.
5. **Matching Team Shirts** — Units sharing a Trait with attached Unit get +1 Guard during your opponent's turn.

## Notes

Unruly may eventually receive an **Assist-style resource mechanic**, where exhausting or already controlling Units helps reduce costs. Exact implementation is not locked.
