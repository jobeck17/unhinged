## Findings

- **There was an AI problem.** Preserving and deploying payoffs substantially improves Florida Man and Magician results, especially in the coverage list. This still does not establish expert play or tournament balance.
- **HOA remains a problem worth human testing.** Improved resource/payout decisions do not materially solve that matchup. Low early Trouble and durable defenders are plausible contributors; that explanation remains an inference pending traces and human play.
- **Ramp is active with improved piloting.** Four-cost-or-higher cards appeared by the third own turn in 114/960 revised-pilot runs. Six-cost-or-higher appeared by the fourth own turn in 36/960 (3.8%). These games can use opposing Stash as well as temporary Stash. Separately, a constructed legal line plays five-cost Hoarder on turn three without opposing Stash payment; that proof is not a frequency estimate.
- **Junk Heap remains slow in these lists.** Three Items assembled in 91/960 original-pilot games and 84/960 revised-pilot games. No game reached five. These denominators include games where Heap was never deployed. The revised pilot favors larger payoffs, so this is not an optimized Heap assembly test.
- **Item starvation is still unresolved.** Seven revised-pilot games reached zero Items across hand, board and discard after a three-Item Heap existed. The metric excludes deck, Stash and stored Items; discard access requires retrieval. There is insufficient evidence to redesign Heap.
- **No balance changes were made.** Keep testing the locked set, especially early ramp, three-Item assembly, supplies remaining after assembly and the HOA matchup.

## Limits and reproduction

Mono matchups only, provisional coverage lists, heuristic pilots, no mulligans. Both trial lists contain 20 Characters, six Actions and 14 Items. The focused list omits Hoarder and Play Dead and changes several copy counts, so list differences are not an Item-quantity experiment. Scurry Away reduces ordinary Trouble Composure loss; the existing Last Straw successful-Trouble rule is preserved.
