# Half-Cost Stash Playtest

**State:** TESTING

This is an isolated LAB experiment. It does not change canonical Unhinged.

## Test rule

When you Stash a card from your hand, it provides Fuel equal to half its printed Cost, rounded down.

Examples:
- Cost 1 → 0 Fuel
- Cost 2 → 1 Fuel
- Cost 3 → 1 Fuel
- Cost 4 → 2 Fuel
- Cost 5 → 2 Fuel
- Cost 6 → 3 Fuel

The second-player setup Stash remains worth 1 Fuel because it was not manually Stashed. Mad Scientist keeps its existing protected-battery resource rule for this test.

## What this is testing

Does tying Stash value to card Cost make the Stash decision more meaningful without making expensive cards automatic Stash choices or low-cost cards feel unusable as resources?

## Failure conditions

Substantially redesign or reject the rule if:
- high-cost cards become automatic Stash choices with little real decision;
- low-cost cards become effectively impossible to Stash because 0–1 Fuel is too punishing;
- early-game Fuel becomes too swingy based on opening-hand costs;
- decks with naturally expensive curves gain a large structural advantage unrelated to their intended identity;
- the added resource arithmetic makes the game meaningfully harder to read at the table.
