# Production Cards

This is the future home for card specifications, approved templating, card IDs, set/season metadata, and production card data.

The Alpha 0.03 pool in [`docs/card-pool/`](../../docs/card-pool/README.md) is a legacy test pool. Do not copy its text into production in bulk: rewrite and verify each card against the current rules, terminology, and card-design principles.

Before a card is production-ready, record at minimum:

- stable card ID and set/season number;
- title and subtitle, where applicable;
- card type, cost, identity, and rules text;
- Power and Guard for Units, when applicable;
- templating and accessibility review status.
