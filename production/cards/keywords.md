## Scope and edge cases

- **Hothead:** Does not Ready it, grant an extra Attack, allow its own Rotate abilities early, or override a cannot-Attack effect.
- **Defiant:** Applies to blocking retaliation. Slowpoke prevents this retaliation if both apply.
- **Explosive:** This is a Defeat trigger and does not damage Leaders, Items, or friendly Characters.
- **Slowpoke:** It may still Attack and may retaliate after surviving a direct Attack.
- **Sucker Punch:** The direct Attack cannot be Blocked and follows normal damage and retaliation. Entry-Turn restrictions still apply unless another effect grants Hothead.
- **Jerry-Rig:** While in Stash, this card is not mechanically an Item. Its face-up state only marks it as temporary Stash.
- **Chicken:** Optional. Returning it ends the Attack before combat damage if it was the direct target.
- **Stubborn:** Protects against both Return and Dismiss once per Round.
- **Bodyguard:** This changes legal direct-attack targeting and mandatory blocking while Ready.
- Multiple instances of the same keyword do not multiply its effect unless a card explicitly says otherwise.
- Tag Me In! can grant Hothead temporarily; it is not an additional printed-keyword Character.

## Shelved mechanics

Sneaky, Cloak, Stack, Step Aside, Overkill are preserved in design history but are not active starting-set mechanics.

## Ordinary vocabulary, not keywords

Rotate, Ready, Attack, Block, Defeat, Sacrifice, Dismiss, Draw, Discard, Return, **deal damage**, and **put damage** are core instructions. Traits grant no behavior on their own.
