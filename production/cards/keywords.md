# Donut Keywords

> September 27, 2026 rebuild status. The current 180-card pool has not yet been audited, so some legacy cards may still print shelved keywords until the production rewrite.

## Current rebuild keywords

| Keyword | Rule | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hothead | This Character may Attack on the Turn it enters play. | Current rebuild keyword |
| Defiant | This Character retaliates when it Blocks even if the incoming Attack Defeats it. | Current rebuild keyword |
| Explosive | When this Character is Defeated, deal 1 damage to each opposing Character. | Current rebuild keyword |
| Slowpoke | This Character does not retaliate when it Blocks. | Current rebuild keyword |
| Sucker Punch | This Character may Attack Ready opposing Characters. | Current rebuild keyword; working name |
| Jerry-Rig | If this Item would go to your discard, you may put it face up and Rotated into your Stash instead. When this card is used to pay a Cost, discard it. | Current rebuild keyword; not yet assigned in legacy pool |

### Scope and edge cases

- **Hothead:** Does not Ready the Character, grant an extra Attack, allow its own Rotate abilities early, or override a cannot-Attack effect.
- **Defiant:** Applies to blocking retaliation. Use the Character's Power immediately before it left play. Slowpoke prevents that retaliation if both apply.
- **Explosive:** A Defeat trigger, not retaliation. It affects opposing Characters only, not Leaders, Items, or friendly Characters.
- **Slowpoke:** The Character may still Attack and may still retaliate after surviving a direct Attack.
- **Sucker Punch:** The direct Attack cannot be Blocked and follows normal damage and retaliation. Entry-Turn restrictions still apply unless another effect grants Hothead.
- **Jerry-Rig:** The replacement is optional. A Jerry-Rigged card is not mechanically an Item while in Stash. It remains face up only to show that it is temporary Stash and will be discarded when spent.

## Shelved for the starting rebuild

**Sneaky, Cloak, Stack, Step Aside, and Overkill** are preserved in design history but are not active starting-set mechanics for the next production pass.

Legacy card data may still contain Sneaky, Cloak, or Stack until the Keep / Rehome / Rewrite / Replace audit is complete.

## Ordinary vocabulary, not keywords

Rotate, Ready, Attack, Block, Defeat, Sacrifice, Dismiss, Draw, Discard, Return, deal damage, and put damage are core instructions.

Traits such as Undead, Rat, Wrestler, Hacker, or Magician grant no behavior on their own. They matter only when a card explicitly refers to them.

Do not add a keyword merely to give every Style an exclusive mechanic.
