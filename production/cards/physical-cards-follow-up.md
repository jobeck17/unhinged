## What to do with the physical cards

**Bush:** Put a Character costing 2 or less from hand face down under it on entry. The opponent sees that *something* is hiding, not its identity. If the opposing player attacks your Leader, the Character may emerge at Attack checkpoint 1, before Blockers are declared. It enters Ready for free and may Block if the Attack is blockable, but its enters-play abilities do not trigger. Reveal and Return it to hand if it is still hidden at the end of the Round or the Bush leaves play. This makes the attack into a bluff and gives the opponent the option to attack elsewhere, wait, or remove the Item. Playing Bush without a Character in hand does not secretly fill it later.

**Hot Potato:** On entry, put the Item beside the opposing Leader. Only the player whose Leader it is beside may use its printed activation, spending a Turn and Rotating one of their Ready Characters to push it to the other Leader. At end of Round it deals 1 damage to whichever Leader it is beside and returns to its Owner's hand. It can be replayed in another Round. The Item never changes owner and never attaches, even when physically next to the other player's Leader. If it leaves play early, its delayed hit does not occur.

**Vacuum:** Recovered from the earlier Jailbroken Robot Vacuum brainstorm: each Attack slides the top card of its owner's deck face down beneath it (if any) and gets +1 Power for that Attack per card collected. The cards stay under it across Rounds. Neither player knows what was sucked up until it leaves play. When Defeated, reveal and Discard them; optionally deal damage to one opposing Character equal to the number of revealed Items. If it leaves by Return or another non-Defeat effect, reveal and Discard the tucked cards with no damage payoff. Its knife attachment belongs in the art/flavor, not a separate keyword. This is a physical stack under one Character, not the unresolved global Stack mechanic.

## First playtest questions

1. Bush: Is paying 2 Fuel and a Turn for the hidden Blocker worth it? Does forbidding enters-play triggers keep its free arrival in check? Does the hidden card return cleanly if Bush is removed?
2. Hot Potato: Is a delayed, reusable 1-damage threat fun to pass, or does it absorb too many Turns? Can both players tell which side it is beside at Round end?
3. Vacuum: How often do one or more Items actually appear in its stack? Is +1 Power per collected card too much after repeated Attacks? Are Return and Defeat cleanup physically obvious?
4. Recheck any decks using P029, P089, or P105 before relying on older deck reports. The saved Florida/HOA duel uses none of them.

Bush and the Vacuum currently carry more rules text than most cards in the pool. A printable card-face test should check whether the cost, hidden-card timing, and cleanup remain legible; if not, move a narrowly defined reminder into the play aid rather than shrinking the text below a readable size.

These cards are deliberately limited to printed local exceptions. General card-backs-as-a-map, hidden Response cards, and the multi-card Stack remain separate future experiments.
