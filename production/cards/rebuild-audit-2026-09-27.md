# Unhinged 180-Card Rebuild Audit — 27 September 2026

> **Status: production triage. This file does not itself change card text.**
>
> Basis: the September 27 full-turn/Stash rules, six Style identities, two-package guideline, Leader passives, keyword decisions, and current brainstorm banks. Every legacy card is evaluated against the new game rather than protected by history.

## Audit legend

- **Keep** — concept and current mechanical core already belong in the new game. Minor wording/balance updates may still happen.
- **Rewrite** — keep the card identity/name, but rebuild meaningful rules text.
- **Rehome** — the card concept belongs more naturally in another Style.
- **Replace** — free the slot for a stronger banked/new concept.
- **Package** — the mechanical package the slot should primarily serve.
- **Role** — why the card exists inside the pool.

## Priority inserts and reserved concepts

These ideas should receive real production slots during the rewrite rather than remaining chat-only concepts.

### Reckless

- **Amateur Electrician** — strong candidate for Self-Damage. Keep the electrical-disaster joke without the darker toaster-in-bathtub reference.
- **Damaged bruiser** — 3–4 Cost, roughly 1/4 baseline; while damaged gets +3 Power. Exact name/stat balance TBD.
- **Clean self-damage enabler** — On Play, may deal 1 damage to a friendly Character. Firework Dad is the leading existing home.
- Preserve Character-only direct damage as a balance valve for Damage Everywhere.

### Momentum

- **Coupon Lady** — move from Stonewall; leading direction is Ramp/discount, with Salvage Big Hand as fallback.
- **Low Hand reset Action** — all players discard their hands, then Draw 3.
- **Catch-up Draw** — if an opponent has more cards in hand, Draw until you have the same number.
- Include threshold cards such as “while you have 2 or fewer cards in hand…” and opponent-large-hand payoffs.
- Momentum should have very little Hothead, possibly none.

### Misdirection

- **School Bully** — Chicken candidate; should also have an annoying board-presence ability that makes the opponent want to attack it.
- **Lady Who's Moving Out Again** — Bounce engine.
- **Magician damaged-Return payoff** — card-level space for Draw based on damage on a Returned Character.
- **Magician damage-transfer marquee** — card-level space to move/put more damage than the Leader baseline allows.
- **Hypnotize / mind control** — prefer “an opposing Character attacks its own Leader” over physically changing control.
- **Inconspicuous Bush** remains banked while tucked/face-down cards are shelved.

### Salvage

- **Hoarder** — locked for Scrounge / Big Hand and should be one of the stronger package cards.
- **Guy Fixing His Trans Am** — Repurpose/Item build-around.
- **Unused Stash Action** — “Rotate up to 3 Ready Stash. Draw a card for each Stash Rotated this way.” May use either player's Stash.
- Every Salvage Item should initially receive **Jerry-Rig** for testing.
- Big Hand should grow primarily through recovery/scrounging rather than generic Draw.

### Stonewall

- **Neighborhood Group Admin** — strong rehome candidate from Misdirection.
- **Terms and Conditions** — strong rehome candidate from Misdirection for forced discard/denial.
- **Copycat package** — opponent performs useful action, Stonewall receives a smaller/mirrored benefit.
- **Freeze/Stall** needs more persistent Characters that Rotate Ready enemies or interfere with normal Readying.
- **Crossing Guard** is the leading Bodyguard candidate.
- Seed Responses here heavily, including at least one carefully costed hard-cancel test.

### Expendable

- **Backyard Wrestling Superfan** — leading home for persistent Wrestler trait support such as granting Hothead.
- Preserve Undead recursion, tag gameplay, sacrifice-to-value, and Defeat triggers as separate but overlapping engines.
- Avoid overloading the package with Reckless-style self-damage; combat damage and sacrifice should remain the main texture.
