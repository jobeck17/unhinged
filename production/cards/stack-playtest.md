# Stack: limited printed test

**Working name:** Stack. **Team Up** and **Pile On** remain possible final names. The [active rulebook](../rules/unhinged-rules.md#stack-two-face-up-character-layers) defines the playable rule; this page records why it appears on only two Characters.

| Top card | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| P037 Reunion Tour Drummer (Momentum, Cost 3, 4/3) | Gains +2 Power on Attack if stacked; the top supplies the combat stats while a useful lower ability stays active. |
| P106 Shopping Cart Golem (Salvage, Cost 2, 2/4) | On Defeat, returns the lower card from discard to hand if it is still there. The attacker cannot wipe both layers permanently without more work. |

A Stack Character can be Played as an ordinary Character or atop one of your own unstacked Characters for its normal Cost. Both layers stay face up, their ability boxes visible, and form one Character. Top Power, Guard, Traits and attack/block restrictions apply; abilities from both layers are active and simultaneous triggers resolve bottom to top. Orientation, existing damage, and Attack history stay with the pile. Defeat and Return normally move **both** cards together. The pile is limited to two layers; no discount, additional fee, or bonus Turn applies.

**First test:** Play these two cards normally and stacked, including with an existing Character with an enters-play or Defeat ability below. Note whether an extra ability is worth spending a Turn and Fuel to combine two bodies. If piles reduce board interaction or cannot be read once Items attach, remove Stack rather than expanding it. [Jailbroken Robot Vacuum](physical-cards-follow-up.md) tucks face-down deck cards out of play; its cargo is never a Stack layer.
