## Deck plans

**Hold My Entire Deck — Florida Man, Gnarly + Wasted.** Use Gas Station Daredevil, Hold My Beer, and Firework Dad to convert survivable damage into Power and Charge. Gym Selfie Guy is a durable target. Helmet and Walk It Off repair damage. Tag Me In returns a damaged body and replaces it, with immediate Attack permission when the returned Character was a Wrestler. Gas Station Pills Readies a Character but does not waive its entry-Round Attack restriction. Florida's ultimate chooses its target before the die roll.

**Your Deck Violates the Bylaws — HOA President, Spiteful + Sketchy.** Keep durable bodies Ready, weaken attackers with Grumpy Old Guy and HOA Enforcement Committee, and repair survivors. Neighborhood Lifeguard and Security Camera suppress threats; using a Character's Rotate ability exposes that Character to direct attacks. Used Ham Sandwich is a consumable repair. Violation Notice finishes damaged opposing Rotated Characters. Save the Leader's own healing activation until opening the Leader to attacks is worth the risk.

Both lists deliberately use 24 Characters, 10 Actions, and 6 Items, with 28 cards in the Leader's Style and 12 in the secondary Style. This is a test control, not a deckbuilding requirement. Every entry is two copies. Both Leaders have 25 Health and sit outside the deck.

## Physical test procedure

Play four games with these unchanged lists: swap decks after each game and alternate the first player. Use normal seven-card opening hands, mulligans, and the Round 1 Draw. For subsequent uncontrolled games restore normal setup War.

Record winner, Rounds, first player, first Charge/ultimate Round, each Leader's regular/ultimate use count, and any Round in which both players had plausible attacks but passed. Mark when a Leader activation's Vulnerability causes damage. Note whether an ultimate is actually exciting, not just available.

The first test questions are whether defensive Charge is too dependent on the opponent, whether the HOA deck's lack of 1-cost Characters matters, whether defensive Discards slow the game excessively, and whether an ultimate worth only 1 damage feels bad after three Charge. Do not alter Health, decklists, Charge threshold, and ultimates simultaneously; change one factor after recording the baseline.
