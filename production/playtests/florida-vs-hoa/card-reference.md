# Card reference — first duel

Two copies of each card below. Exact text from production pool 0.1-donut.3. Leader rules are in [leaders.md](leaders.md).

## Florida Man

### P001 — Feral Chihuahua

2 copies · Gnarly · Character · Cost 1 · 2/1

Traits: Animal.

While attacking a Character with Guard 3 or more, this gets +2 Power.

### P002 — Gas Station Daredevil

2 copies · Gnarly · Character · Cost 2 · 3/2

Traits: Daredevil.

When this attacks, you may deal 1 damage to it. If you do, it gets +2 Power for this Attack.

### P003 — Firework Dad

2 copies · Gnarly · Character · Cost 3 · 3/4

Traits: Parent, Daredevil.

Rotate: Deal 1 damage to another of your Characters. If it survives, it gets +2 Power this Round.

### P004 — Bachelorette Party

### P005 — Unsupervised Toddler

2 copies · Gnarly · Character · Cost 2 · 1/3

Traits: Kid.

Explosive. When this attacks, reveal the top card of your deck. If its Cost is 3 or more, this gets +3 Power for this Attack. Put the revealed card on the bottom of your deck.

### P172 — Tag Me In!

2 copies · Wasted · Action · Cost 2

Return one of your damaged Characters to your hand. If you do, you may Play a different Character from your hand with equal or lower Cost without paying its Cost. If the returned Character was a Wrestler, the new Character gains Hothead this Round.

### P178 — Folding Chair

2 copies · Wasted · Item · Cost 1

### P179 — Definitely Safe Helmet

2 copies · Wasted · Item · Cost 2
