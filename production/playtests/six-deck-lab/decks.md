# Six mono-Style 40-card baseline decks

> **September 28, 2026 rebuild baseline.** These decks are deliberately mono-Style. Their job is to prove that each Style, both of its major packages, and its current Leader passive function without help from a secondary Style. They are not optimized tournament lists and have not yet been balance-tuned.

All decks contain exactly **40 cards**, use no more than **4 copies** of a card, and use only the Leader's Style. The Leader remains outside the deck at 25 Health.

## Testing order

Start with matchups that stress different axes rather than mirror-style games:

1. **Florida Man vs. HOA President** — pressure versus denial; tests Sucker Punch, Ready-step tax, and Response tension.
2. **Washed-Up Rock Star vs. Trash Baron** — economy race; tests extra Stash, discounts, Jerry-Rig, and opposing-Stash spending.
3. **Birthday Party Magician vs. Backyard Wrestler** — movement versus replacement; tests Bounce, Tag Out, On Play reuse, and recursion.
4. Rotate all six through the full round robin after obvious rules failures are fixed.

For the first pass, do **not** tune cards from one strange game. Log repeated patterns across pilots and starting-player positions.
