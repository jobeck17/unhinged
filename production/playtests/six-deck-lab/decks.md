# Six complete 40-card decks

All six decks use exactly two Styles, at most two copies per ID, and a Leader outside the deck. Card text lives in [the active pool](../../cards/cards.json). These are playtest lists, not established balance results. Florida Man and HOA retain their original matchup lists for reproducibility. HOA is an advanced defensive challenge; start with Rock Star versus Wrestler or Magician versus Trash Baron.
