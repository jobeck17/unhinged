# Revision 12 matchup baseline

> September 29, 2026 · Heuristic simulation snapshot

Revision 12 combines the tested Rock Star hand-gate package, cross-Style Return counterplay, HOA's Rotated-board Return Action, and Backyard Wrestler's Stash-scaled Tag Out.

## Method

- Six mono-Style 40-card decks.
- 30,000-game field equivalent: 2,000 games per unordered matchup, with both starting orders represented.
- Ten pairings unchanged by Tag Out are carried forward from the immediately preceding corrected full-field run.
- The five Wrestler pairings were rerun after correcting Tag Out so it triggers only during Wrestler's own Turn.
- Same-harness matchup comparisons are the strongest signal. Exact percentages remain heuristic and are not a substitute for human play.

## Tag Out engagement

In the 10,000 rerun games involving Wrestler:

- Tag Out triggered **2.53 times/game**.
- **1.35/game** put a qualifying Character directly into play.
- **1.18/game** put the revealed non-qualifying card into hand.
- Hit rate: **53.3%**.
- Tag Out entrants made **1.27 Hothead attacks/game**.

## Current read

The field now has a visible counter-web rather than one universally dominant deck. Trash remains strong overall but loses to HOA; Rock is strong into Magician but loses to Trash; Wrestler has a favorable Florida matchup but is vulnerable to HOA and Trash. Human testing should now focus on whether these matchup textures are fun rather than forcing every deck toward exactly 50%.
